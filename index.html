Drone Aware

Part 107 pre-flight airspace and weather checks, in one place.In development

Before you fly, Drone Aware shows what the airspace, the weather and the rules say about this exact spot, then gives you the checklists to follow.

What makes it different. Airspace checks are free from several established apps. Drone Aware is built around everything a pilot does around the check: the height limits near structures, the weather minimums and the paperwork.

Features
  • Live airspace class and altitude ceilings from the FAA UAS Facility MapBuilt
  • Weather checked against Part 107 minimums, from two public weather sourcesBuilt
  • A structure-height calculator based on 14 CFR 107.51(b)(2)Built
  • 11 printable field checklistsBuilt
  • Delete your account and saved data from inside the appBuilt
  • Cloud check history and branded PDF reportsPlanned

Be part of it. We take safety-critical field tools from idea to a working product. If you have a workflow like this one, we would like to hear about it.

Drone Aware is not approved or endorsed by the FAA.

Give it an engine data log and three AI reviewers read it, each from a different angle: fuel, mechanical and drivability.

What makes it different. It is built to say "cannot assess" instead of guessing. When a sensor is missing, the code stops the AI from calling that channel normal.

Features
  • Reads Speeduino and Subaru/Atlas ECU log exportsBuilt

Our process

An example workflow

This is one example of how a build can move from first draft to live release. It is not a fixed workflow for every project. Each project is planned around what it actually needs, and the path is tailored to fit.

How we use AI

AI tools help us draft and move faster. A person decides what gets built, reviews everything the AI produces, and signs off on every release. Each build goes through our 11-step audit, and you receive the full source files, not just the finished product.

The 11-step audit

Foundation

  1. Structural baseline
  2. Reliability pass
  3. Accuracy verification
  4. Consistency check

People and protection

  1. Functionality and ease of use
  2. Security
  3. Accessibility

Real-world conditions

  1. Performance under load
  2. Edge cases and error states
  3. Cross-device and cross-browser testing
  4. Data integrity and validation
