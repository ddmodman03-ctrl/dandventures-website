Privacy policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what you type into our forms, plus basic technical data our web host records when you visit.
  • We use it to reply to you and to run the early-access and pilot programs you sign up for.
  • We do not sell your information, and this site runs no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • Please do not send health, patient or other sensitive information through our forms.
  • You can ask us to delete your information at any time by email.

1. Who we are

This site is run by DanD Development and Ventures, a DBA of Oi8wat Holdings LLC. We are a development and ventures studio based in Texas, USA. You can reach us at danddevelopementandventures@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

What you give us. The contact form on this site asks for:

  • your name and email address;
  • what you are interested in, such as a build, early access, a pilot, funding or a partnership;
  • the project you mean, if any, which is filled in for you when you use a button on a project; and
  • your message.

The same form handles every kind of request, including early-access, pilot, funding and partnership requests.

What our web host records. When you visit, our hosting provider records ordinary technical data in its server logs, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and the time. We do not use analytics or advertising tools, and we do not set advertising or analytics cookies.

Fonts. Our pages load the Inter typeface from Google Fonts. Loading it sends your IP address and browser details to Google.

3. What not to send us

Do not include patient or health information, financial account numbers or government ID numbers in any form on this site. If you take part in a pilot and want to share project materials, such as engine data logs or clinical content, we will arrange that separately and in writing. Please do not send those materials through the site.

4. How we use it

  • To reply to your message and to talk with you about working together.
  • To add you to the early-access or pilot list for the product you chose, and to email you about that product.
  • To keep the site secure and filter out spam.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not send marketing that is unrelated to what you asked about.

5. Who handles it

We use a small number of service providers to run the site and read your messages:

  • Netlify hosts the site and handles the forms. It stores form submissions in its own database and checks every submission for spam using a filtering service called Akismet.
  • Google delivers form notifications to our email inbox through Gmail, and supplies the Inter typeface through Google Fonts.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising. We may disclose it if the law requires, or to protect our rights or the safety of others. If our business is sold or reorganized, your information may pass to the new owner, who would need to honor this policy.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your messages and sign-ups while we are working with you or may work with you. We delete them within 12 months after our last contact, unless the law requires us to keep them or you ask us to delete them sooner. Our hosting provider may keep technical logs and backups for its own periods, which we do not control.

7. Your choices

Email us at danddevelopementandventures@gmail.com to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to leave an early-access or pilot list. We may ask you to confirm it is really you. We normally reply within 30 days.

8. Security

This site is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection, and we limit who can read form submissions. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email danddevelopementandventures@gmail.com.
